# Code of conduct

Catfish Lab welcomes disagreement about claims, designs, and evidence. It does not welcome contempt
for the people making them.

Be specific, curious, and proportionate. Critique observable behavior rather than identity or
ability. Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and human authority. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of secrets are not acceptable in
project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation to protect this standard.
Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact route described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports should be handled discreetly, fairly, and without retaliation.
