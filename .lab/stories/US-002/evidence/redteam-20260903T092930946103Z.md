# Red Team Report

## Confirmed defects

- **Observation:** `open_profile()` awaits `provider.private_record()` without capturing or later validating `_round_generation`. If `abandon_round()` runs while that await is pending, the controller returns to `BROWSING`, but the delayed `open_profile()` can subsequently call `RoundState.open_profile()` successfully and reactivate the abandoned round.
- **Inference:** A delayed profile-open callback can override a newer Browse action and navigate the player back into a chat they explicitly abandoned.
- **Recommendation:** Capture the round generation before awaiting the provider and reject the result if it changes. Add a barrier-based test: start `open_profile()`, wait until `private_record()` is blocked, call `abandon_round()`, release the provider, and assert that opening fails and the controller remains cleanly `BROWSING`.

## Suspected defects

- **Observation:** Browser click-through testing remains explicitly untested; startup and HTTP 200 do not exercise rendered controls or navigation.
- **Uncertainty:** The complete browse → chat → bank → result flow has not been demonstrated through the pinned Flet client.
- **Recommendation:** Record successful and failed rounds through a real browser or Flet client.

## Missing tests

- A delayed `open_profile()` test proving `abandon_round()` cannot be undone by a stale provider response.
- An overlapping UI-event test covering Open profile followed immediately by Browse.
- A pinned-runtime browser test covering the complete successful round.
- The same browser flow with an incorrect answer and visible `RECOVERY FAILED`.
- A browser assertion that the seeded portrait is served and rendered.
- A UI-boundary inspection proving rendered controls, callback payloads, and serialized public values contain no private recovery record.

## Design concerns

- **Observation:** Stale-result protection is implemented for `send_message()` but not for `open_profile()`, even though both span asynchronous provider calls and mutate round state afterward.
- **Inference:** The controller’s asynchronous mutation contract is inconsistent and future awaited operations may repeat this defect.
- **Recommendation:** Apply one generation-token or serialization policy to every state-changing operation that crosses an await boundary.

## Areas tested without finding a defect

- **Observation:** Concurrent chat operations are serialized, preventing the configured message limit from being exceeded.
- **Observation:** Pending chat replies are rejected after recovery begins or the round generation changes.
- **Observation:** Recovery validation is deterministic and requires both accepted answers.
- **Observation:** Public and private persona models are distinct, and `PublicProfile` rejects recovery data.
- **Observation:** Provider failures and domain transition failures are translated to `GameControllerError`.
- **Observation:** Invalid chat-route identities and invalid Bank phases are guarded.
- **Observation:** Result labels explicitly distinguish successful and failed recovery.
- **Observation:** Supplied evidence reports linting, formatting, Lab doctor, 43 tests, portrait integrity, and API-key-free runtime startup passing.
