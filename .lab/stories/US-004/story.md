# User Story

## Statement

As a reader, I want a short original poem about Gilgamesh so that the ancient hero's search for
meaning is expressed with clarity and emotional force.

## User value

A concise, readable creative work that can stand on its own without requiring knowledge of the Lab.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The result is recognizably a poem rather than an explanation.
- [ ] It is short: no more than sixteen lines.
- [ ] It evokes Gilgamesh, mortality, friendship, or the walls of Uruk.
- [ ] It is original and avoids pretending to quote an ancient translation.

## Out of scope

Literary criticism, a full retelling of the epic, or claims of historical translation.

## Unknowns

No tone or poetic form was specified; restrained free verse is a reasonable default.

## AI experiment required?

no

## Notes

The final poem should be shown prominently, not buried inside process narration.
