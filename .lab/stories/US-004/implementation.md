+# Gilgamesh
+
+He raised the walls of Uruk high,
+as if stone could forbid the grave.
+Then Enkidu fell, and the king
+heard death breathing in his own name.
+
+He crossed the dark for endless life,
+returning with empty hands—
+yet dawn burned gold upon the walls,
+and friendship outlived the man.
