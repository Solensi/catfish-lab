# Why Catfish exists

Catfish is not another agent and it is not a project-management system. It is a thin, local protocol
around AI-assisted project work: preserve the request, separate responsibilities, expose failures,
and retain evidence that survives the conversation.

## Use it when

- another person may need to understand why a change happened;
- a model's confident completion claim deserves an independent challenge;
- the work contains a real uncertainty that can be tested;
- you switch model providers but want one stable project history;
- failures and abandoned paths are evidence, not terminal noise.

## Do not use it when

- the work is disposable and its history has no value;
- a deterministic formatter or one-line command already settles the question;
- you need a hosted team tracker rather than repository-local evidence;
- you are unwilling to keep sensitive input out of prompts and artifacts.

## What the cast buys you

The Product Steward narrows the promise. The Builder changes the project. The Red Team searches for
a reproducible violation. The Judge asks whether “done” was demonstrated. The Archivist turns the
sealed trail into a readable account. On FULL work, the Scientist, Architect, and blind Heretic keep
an attractive first answer from becoming the only answer.

They may share one foundation model, but they do not share hidden conversational memory. Their
claims cross role boundaries only through visible artifacts.

## The irreducible bargain

LIGHT costs one durable request, one coding handoff, independent review, and one final acceptance.
Removing any of those would give up scope, actual execution, independent challenge, or human
authority. Everything else is optional depth.
